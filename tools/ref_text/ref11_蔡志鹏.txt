Interactive Q&A dual-channel semantic retrieval based on Transformer
Cai Zhipeng, Huang Jin, Tao Xiongjie, Hu Bin
(Macau University of Science and Technology, Macau 999078, China)
Abstract： -
In allusion to the issues of one sided semantic feature extraction and insufficient retrieval accuracy in interactive
- - - -
question answering semantic retrieval, a method of Transformer based dual channel semantic retrieval for interactive question
-
answering (Q&A) is proposed. A dual channel semantic matching retrieval model is built, which includes Transformer encoding
-
layers based on dual channel attention mechanism, interactive attention layers, and semantic matching retrieval layers. The
-
semantic features and positional features of question text and answer text are extracted by means of dual channel attention
mechanism. After multiple rounds of semantic interaction and fusion, the answer texts that match the question text are sorted and
-
fed back to user according to the similarity of the dual channel semantic features, so as to realize the interactive Q&A dual
channel semantic retrieval. The experimental results show that, in the retrieval tasks involving 10 to 110 target contents, the
Kappa coefficient of the retrieval results obtained by the proposed method remains above 0.95, demonstrating outstanding
-
retrieval accuracy and stability. This method can effectively mine dual channel semantic features of interactive Q&A and improve
the response precision of the system.
